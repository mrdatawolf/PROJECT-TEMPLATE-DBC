Concretely, a contract document is a markdown file with a fixed shape:
purpose, scope — included and excluded, explicitly — actors, inputs and
outputs, preconditions, required behavior, postconditions and invariants,
failure behavior, interfaces, validation requirements, and open
questions. Open questions is the section everyone skips on a normal spec
doc. Don't skip it here — it's where the real risk lives, and I've got a
production example to prove it.
