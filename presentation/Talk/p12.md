## Where the vocabulary comes from

[Slide 12 — Design by Contract origin]

This is borrowed, deliberately, from Bertrand Meyer's Design by Contract,
Eiffel, 1980s. A routine and its caller share a contract like a legal one:
a precondition — what the caller must guarantee going in; a postcondition
— what the routine guarantees coming out, if the precondition held; an
invariant — a consistency property every operation has to restore at
observable boundaries. Break the contract, and it's immediately clear
whose bug it is. That last property is the one I actually want — not the
Eiffel syntax, the accountability.
