## The loop this system runs

[Slide 2b — "Think once. Execute fast. Repeat."]

Contract → fast, disposable execution → working software → accept or
reject → next contract. That's the loop. The pipeline I'm about to walk
through is one pass through that loop, zoomed in. The pipeline isn't the
point — the loop is. Keep that in the back of your head, because it's easy
to get absorbed in the mechanics of the pipeline and lose the thread.
