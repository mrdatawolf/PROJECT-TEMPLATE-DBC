## What you actually get

[Slide 26 — the payoff]

Bounded scope — approval authorizes exactly what's in the task, nothing
adjacent. Reviewable diffs — there's a contract to check the diff
against, not just vibes. Ambiguity surfaces early, as an open question,
not as a surprise in production. Durable knowledge — decisions survive
the chat window that made them. Accountable roles — when something
breaks, the responsible boundary is clear. And it's just files — markdown
and directories. Grep it, diff it, review it in a PR, like everything
else in your repo already is.
