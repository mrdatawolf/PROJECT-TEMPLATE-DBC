[Slide 14 — the reframe]

So: the routine/caller relationship becomes the model for the human/agent
relationship. Explicit expectations, verifiable results, clear
responsibility. This is the actual rule set — seven rules, and every one
of them is enforceable by a human reading a diff, not by a runtime:

1. Understand and design meaningful changes before implementing them.
2. Do not begin implementation without an approved task.
3. Implement approved behavior without silently expanding scope.
4. Escalate material ambiguity to the human.
5. Keep lasting knowledge in the repository, not chat history.
6. Independent review is distinct from implementation.
7. Do not continue beyond the responsibility assigned to your role.
