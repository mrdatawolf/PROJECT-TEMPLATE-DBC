[Slide 23, 24 — proof at scale]

Does this actually work, or is it a nice diagram? MeshtasticForeman is an
Electron desktop app plus daemon plus web UI, actively being rebuilt under
this exact process right now. As of this talk: eleven accepted contracts,
four recorded ADRs, forty-eight completed tasks, six currently in flight
across approved and review. Every one of those forty-eight completions has
a recorded handoff and an independent review behind it — not just a
merged PR. And it's not just a tally — open `tasks/completed/` and it's a
timeline, forty-eight files, same shape every time, each one openable into
why it actually happened. Task 45 is a good example: "Fix Nodes tab 'Last
Heard' sort so the displayed column matches the sort key" — proposed by
Claude off an investigation of a reported screenshot, not a guess,
approved by a human with a date attached, then desired outcome, context,
scope, plan, acceptance criteria, validation, handoff, review, and
acceptance all in the same file.
